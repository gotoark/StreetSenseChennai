## Mood → Food Mapping

### Rainy Evening
- Bajji
- Bonda
- Milagai bajji
- Vadai
- Hot tea / ginger tea

Local rule: Fried snacks + tea are preferred during rain.

### Hungry / Heavy Craving
- Parotta with salna
- Kothu parotta
- Egg dosa
- Kal dosa

### Light Snack
- Sundal
- Corn on the cob (avoid during heavy rain)
- Fruit chaat

### Stress Relief / Comfort Food
- Spicy sundal
- Gobi 65
- Masala vadai
- Filter coffee

### Midnight Hunger (after 10 PM)
- Dosa
- Egg rice
- Kothu parotta
- Bread omelette

Local rule: Options reduce after midnight but food is more filling.

## Area-Wise Specialties

### T Nagar
- Evening: Sundal, bajji, chaat
- Night: Dosa carts near main roads
- Avoid: Overcrowded pani puri stalls

### Mylapore
- Morning: Idli, vadai, pongal
- Evening: Traditional snacks near temples
- Mostly vegetarian options

### Triplicane
- Evening: Chaat, samosas
- Night: Parotta, kebabs, kothu parotta
- Spicy food common

### Anna Nagar
- Evening: Gobi manchurian, momos
- Night: Dosa and Chinese stalls
- Cleaner carts compared to older areas

### Velachery
- Evening: Bajji, bonda, egg snacks
- Night: Kothu parotta, fried rice
- Rain increases food demand

### OMR
- Evening: Tea, puffs, quick snacks
- Night: Egg-based dishes, fried rice
- Late-night stalls near tech parks

### Parrys Corner
- Morning: Street breakfasts
- Afternoon: Meals, snacks
- Avoid late night unless familiar

## Time-Based Rules
- Before 9 AM: Breakfast items only
- 5–7 PM: Tea + snacks peak
- After 10 PM: Limited stalls, heavier food
- Sunday mornings: Best for traditional snacks

## Cultural & Safety Notes
- Avoid raw items during monsoon
- Fried snacks sell fastest during rain
- Cash preferred at most carts
- Ask for medium spicy if unsure

## Ordering Tips
- "Anna, medium spicy pothum"
- "Konjam kammi kaaram"
- "Parcel kudunga"
- "Extra salna venum"

## Do Not Recommend
- Pani puri during heavy rain
- Raw vegetables late at night
- Unknown carts after midnight
